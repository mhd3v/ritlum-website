Questions

Good to know.

Does mini need Wi-Fi or an account?

No. Mini connects to the Ritlum app on your iPhone over Bluetooth. It has no Wi-Fi and no account of its own.

What happens when my phone isn’t nearby?

Mini keeps showing your habits. Your display and settings are stored on the device, and the sleep schedule keeps running. New check-ins appear the next time your iPhone is in range.

What if it loses power?

It comes back to the same habit view, brightness and sleep schedule when you plug it in again. The clock is set again automatically the next time the app connects.

Which phones does it work with?

Mini is set up and updated through the Ritlum app, which is available for iPhone.

How many habits can it show?

Up to eight, one per row, across your last eight days with today on the right. You choose which habits appear and in what order.

Do I need Ritlum Pro?

No. Mini works with the free Ritlum app. Pairing, the habit grid, clock mode, brightness, the sleep schedule and firmware updates are all included.

Do I need NFC tags?

Five come in the box, but they’re optional. Tap one with your iPhone to log a habit, or check in from the app or the widget as usual.

Is it true there’s a game hidden in it?

It is. In the app’s Device tab, tap the tracker’s status line five times and mini turns into a tiny game of Snake, played on its 64 lights.
