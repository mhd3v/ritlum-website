Three kinds of goals

Check it. Count it.
Time it.

Some habits are done or not done. Others have a number or a duration. Ritlum tracks all three, on a daily, weekly or monthly schedule.

Check-off

One tap marks it done for the day. Meditate, take your vitamins, make the bed.

Count

Set a daily target and add to it as you go. Eight glasses of water, twenty pages, ten thousand steps.

Timer

Start a session and let it run in the background. Twenty minutes of reading, an hour of practice.

NFC check-ins

Tap a tag.
Habit logged.

Stick an NFC tag where the habit happens: the kettle, your running shoes, the book on your nightstand. Hold your iPhone to it and the habit is logged, without hunting for the app.

Optionally, connect a Ritlum mini to see your habits light up on your desk as you log them.

Stats and history

See what’s
working.

Streaks tell you about today. Stats tell you about the month: which habits are holding, which are slipping, and which days of the week go best.

  • Overview

  • Trends

    Your completion rate over time, week by week, with the days you missed.

  • History

    Every day you’ve tracked, with what you logged and when.

The Tracker tab

Set up your tracker,
your way.

If you have a Ritlum tracker on your desk, the app is where you configure it. Pair it once over Bluetooth, then decide what it shows and when.

Pick the habits

Choose up to eight habits and drag them into the order you want, or mirror your Home screen. A live preview shows the tracker as it looks right now.

Brightness and clock

Turn the lights up or down, or switch to clock mode. Firmware updates install from here too, over Bluetooth.

Sleep schedule

Set when the tracker dims at night and when it wakes. It keeps to the schedule even when your phone is elsewhere.

Questions

Questions and answers

Is Ritlum free?

Yes. The free version includes three habits and one NFC tag. Ritlum Pro adds unlimited habits, full stats and trends and unlimited NFC tags, as a monthly or yearly subscription or a one-time purchase.

Do I need an account?

No. Ritlum works without signing up, and your habits stay on your iPhone. If you want a backup or sync between devices, you can turn on cloud sync with Sign in with Apple.

What kinds of habits can I track?

Habits can repeat daily, weekly or monthly. Each one is a check-off, a count with a target, or a timer.

How does NFC logging work?

Link an NFC tag to a habit in the app and stick it where the habit happens. Hold your iPhone to the tag, tap the notification that appears, and the habit is logged for today.

Does it have Home Screen widgets?

Yes. One widget shows your habit grid and another shows how many habits you’ve completed today.

Do I need Ritlum mini to use the app?

No. The app is a complete habit tracker by itself. Ritlum mini is an optional desk display that shows the same grid in light.

Is there an Android version?

Not yet. Ritlum is only available for iPhone, on iOS 17 or later.
