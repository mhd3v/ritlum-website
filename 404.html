NFC tag

This tag checks off a habit

It’s linked to a habit in Ritlum, the habit tracker for iPhone. Tap it with the app installed and the habit is logged for today.

  1. Download Ritlum from the App Store.
  2. Sign in with the account the tag was set up on, so your habits sync.
  3. Tap the tag again.
